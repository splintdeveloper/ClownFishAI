Problem Solver

Two modes in one tool. AI Writer generates full essays, paragraphs, and responses from a prompt — adjust tone and length to match any assignment. Problem Solver goes through your doc question by question, generating answers you can auto-type one at a time.

AI Checker

Two modes in one tool. AI Check scores your text 0–100 for AI likelihood using the same signals as GPTZero, with a full breakdown of what was flagged. Fact Check scans every claim in your writing and returns a true / false / uncertain verdict for each one.

Rewriter

Paste AI-generated text and get back something that reads like you wrote it. Adjustable from a light polish all the way to a full sentence-level rewrite. Upload samples of your own writing to match your personal style.

Summarize

Condense any assignment, article, or document into a brief, standard, or detailed summary — without reading the whole thing. Load from a page with one click or paste any text.
