Help & FAQ
Getting Started
Why aren't the AI features working?
You need a free API key. Click Settings and paste your key there. All AI tools use it to process your text.
How do I get a free API key?
Go to console.groq.com, create a free account, and generate an API key. It's completely free — no credit card needed. In Settings, make sure the provider is set to Groq and paste the key.
Can I use OpenAI instead?
Yes. In Settings, switch the provider to OpenAI and paste your OpenAI API key. OpenAI requires a paid account.
Important Disclaimer
ClownFish AI is a personal productivity tool. Do not submit AI-generated content as your own work in academic, professional, or legal contexts. Doing so may violate your school's academic integrity policy or professional standards. Use responsibly.
How Each Tool Works
Auto Typer
Pastes text by simulating real keystrokes — useful for fields that block paste. Set your speed (WPM) and randomness, click Start, then click the target field on any webpage. The extension types the text for you character by character.
AI Checker
Analyzes text for signs of AI generation. Paste any text and it returns a 0–100 score, a verdict, and specific signals it detected (like low sentence burstiness or AI clichés).
Rewriter
Rewrites text to sound more human. Paste AI-generated text, choose a humanization level (1 = subtle, 10 = heavy), and it rewrites it to vary sentence length, remove AI clichés, and add natural rhythm.
Problem Solver — AI Writer tab
Writes content from a prompt or assignment. Paste the instructions, choose a length and tone, and it generates a human-sounding draft. Use Advanced Write for a 3-step outline process.
Problem Solver — Problem Solver tab
Goes through your assignment question by question, generating an AI answer for each one. Edit the answer if you like, then auto-type it and move to the next. Great for worksheets, tables, or any doc with lots of questions.
Citations
Suggests citation sources for claims in your text. Paste your text, choose MLA / APA / Chicago style, and it identifies claims and suggests authoritative sources. Always verify sources before submitting.
AI Checker — Fact Check tab
Checks each factual claim in your text against known sources. Returns a verdict (true / false / uncertain / opinion) for each claim. Use Highlight to mark questionable claims directly on the page. Found inside the AI Checker tool.
Other Questions
Summarize
Summarizes text you paste or load from a page. Choose Brief (2–3 sentences), Standard (1 paragraph), or Detailed (key points list). Great for quickly understanding an assignment or condensing something you just wrote.
Is my text sent anywhere?
Text is sent directly to the AI provider (Groq or OpenAI) using your own API key. ClownFish AI does not store or see your text.
Why does Auto Typer close the popup?
It needs to close so you can click on the target field in the webpage. The typing happens in the background — just click where you want the text typed.
